---
name: X0(10) source boundary
description: What the González-Jiménez--Lario R4(10) supplementary tables do and do not establish.
---

Treat González-Jiménez--Lario Proposition 17 and Table 31 only as a
classification of the Faltings-minimal vertex in quadratic twists of an
`R₄(10)` isogeny graph. They do not exclude Frey curves from `X₀(10)`.

**Why:** The historical repository citation pointed to an unrelated 2010
Acta Arithmetica item. The actual source is the supplementary material to
the 2025 preprint *Faltings elliptic curves in twisted Q-isogeny classes*.
Its Tables 28--30 record local data for existing graph members, and
Proposition 17 uses that data to identify Faltings-minimal vertices.

**How to apply:** Do not add or restore an unconditional no-Frey theorem
from these tables. Such a theorem needs a separate Frey-to-graph placement,
individual 2-adic valuations, the coprimality hypotheses used, and a proved
incompatibility with every applicable local row. For the zero-point quotient,
use the factor `a² − 14ab + b² = (a+b)² − 16ab`; in the C-even case this
gives profile `(8−2r,16−4r)`. The superficially similar `−10` formula creates
false Table 30 survivors.
