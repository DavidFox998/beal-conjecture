\section{Lean formalization --- explicit types}\label{sec:formal}

Every theorem in the four finals carries an explicit type. Lean~$4.12$
rejects \texttt{theorem foo := bar}. The required opens are
\texttt{open Nat Finset Classical}. Typical types are
$\mathbb{N}$, $\mathrm{Prop}$, $\mathrm{Finset}\,\mathbb{N}$, and
\texttt{Fintype}. Proofs use \texttt{decide} (or \texttt{norm\_num}
on $\Q$), never \texttt{native\_decide}, so
\texttt{Lean.ofReduceBool} does not appear.

surfaces together with \path{Conditional32Newform.Bridge} and
\path{RequiresTateMazurRibetFor32.Doc}.  The bridge proves
\[

 v_{29}(\Delta)=26v_{29}(B+3),\qquad

as well as $928/29=32$ and $32\cdot29=928$.  Its theorem
\path{has_newform_at_level_32_of_assumptions} takes exactly
\texttt{hMazur}, \texttt{hTate}, and \texttt{hRibet}.  The result is
\path{HasNewformAtLevel32}, a proposition-valued evidence record.  Wiles/BCDT
modularity, the geometric mod-$13$ representation and absolute
irreducibility, Tate/N\'eron and cyclotomic-inertia theory, genuine Ribet
lowering, identification with \texttt{32a1}, and complete Kraus elimination
remain explicit.

\paragraph{Forward Tate closure.}
The subsequent module \path{TateCurve.Neron32_928InertiaQ13} projects the
exact symbol $I_{26k}$ from Tate-algorithm evidence, the $32/928$ split from

\paragraph{Forward Mazur closure.}
The subsequent module \path{MazurCurve.X0_13FreyJBorel} keeps the
two-constructor Fricke cusp table distinct from the infinitely many rational
points on the genus-zero parameter line. It projects the exact Fricke

curve-attached Frey data. Its no-rational-$13$-isogeny theorem consumes both
the explicit Borel/Fricke equivalence and the Frey-specific global exclusion.
Neither genus and cusp numerals nor image-cardinality numerals are used
backwards to construct moduli, geometric torsion, a Galois representation,
or Borel containment.

\paragraph{Forward Ribet closure.}
The subsequent module \path{RibetCurve.Ribet32a1KrausA29} takes constructed
modularity, residual-representation, irreducibility, inertia, conductor,
N\'eron, residual-eigenform, and \texttt{32a1} data. It projects those data
in the mathematical direction and keeps the good-reduction
$a_{29}=-10$ mismatch distinct from complete bad-reduction Kraus
elimination. The independent numerical surface includes the discriminant
and valuation identities, $928/29=32$, $32\cdot29=928$,
$\lvert\SL_2(\F_{13})\rvert=2184$, and $48<2184$; none of those numerals
inhabits a geometric or automorphic boundary.

\paragraph{Forward Kolyvagin closure.}
The subsequent module
\path{KolyvaginCurve.KolyvaginL_OmegaSel2Fintype} keeps the analytic and
two-descent routes explicit. Constructed $L$-functions, positive periods,
and normalized-value identifications precede Kato--Kolyvagin. Constructed
Selmer exact sequences, exact cardinality identifications, and
Mordell--Weil finite generation precede the singleton-Selmer implication.
The displayed values $1/3$, $1/7$, $\lvert\Sel_2\rvert=1$, and
$2^{\dim}=1$ never construct those inputs. Actual \texttt{Fintype}
witnesses provide the finite-point endpoint while the nonzero torsion points
continue to refute the older \texttt{Subsingleton} encoding.
