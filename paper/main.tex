\begin{abstract}
The Beal equation $A^x+B^y=C^z$ with $x,y,z>2$ is studied here only
for the signature $(4,4,13)$. The associated Frey curve
$E:y^2=x(x-A^4)(x+B^4)$ has displayed conductor $928=2^5\cdot 29$,
N\'eron types $I_0^*$ at $2$ and $I_8$ at $29$, invariants $c_4$ and
$\Delta$, and valuations $v_2(\Delta)=6$, $v_{29}(\Delta)=8$. This
draft records the Lean~4.12 structural verification of four honest
formal surfaces and their conditional integration. Mazur:
$\lvert\SL_2(\F_{13})\rvert=2184$,
$48<2184$, $13=2^2+3^2$, $288/48=6$, cusp Finset card~$2$, genus~$0$
(so $X_0(13)(\Q)$ is infinite). Ribet: $928/29=32$, matching Finset
$\emptyset$ of card~$0$, displayed $\dim S_2(\Ga_0(32))=1$ (LMFDB
\texttt{32a1}), $\dim S_2(\Ga_0(16))=0$, Sturm $48$ and $8$.
Kolyvagin: rank-$0$ display on Cremona \texttt{26a1}/\texttt{26b1}
with $\lvert\Sel_2\rvert=1$, $3\cdot 7=21$, $L/\cO=1/3$ and $1/7$,
and $\mathrm{Nonempty}(\mathrm{Fintype}\,\alpha)\to\mathrm{Finite}\,\alpha$.
The continued Tate boundary proves the discriminant and valuation numerics,
records $I_{26k}$ for $k=v_{29}(B+3)$ only as the output of actual
Tate-algorithm evidence, and orders N\'eron $32/928$, cyclotomic
$q^{1/13}$ inertia, and mod-$13$ unramifiedness strictly forward. The
continued Mazur boundary likewise orders point-level $X_0(13)$ data, the
exact Fricke function, attached Frey $j=c_4^3/\Delta$, Borel reducibility,
and global exclusion strictly forward. The integrated bridge
derives proposition-valued level-$32$ evidence from exactly three explicit
Mazur, Tate, and Ribet assumptions; it does not construct an unconditional
Mathlib newform. Theorems use explicit types and \texttt{decide}, not
\texttt{native\_decide}. Software version DOI
\texttt{10.5281/zenodo.22910834}; concept DOI
\texttt{10.5281/zenodo.22379293}.
\end{abstract}
