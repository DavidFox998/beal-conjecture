\begin{abstract}
We study $A^4+B^4=(B+3)^{13}$, of signature $(4,4,13)$.
Fourth-power residues modulo $16$ exclude $B\not\equiv14\pmod{16}$.
For $352$ named remaining rows below two million, successive
non-residue tests at $53$, $29$, and $109$ exclude $266$, $77$,
and $9$ rows. This list is not an exhaustive census.
This records the general Beal conjecture succinctly: for positive
$x,y,z\in\mathbb N$ and natural exponents $p,q,r\ge3$,
$x^p+y^q=z^r$ entails a prime dividing all three bases.
The forward wrapper returns \texttt{data.bealTheorem.commonPrime}
from the explicit proof input \texttt{BealFinalData.bealTheorem}
(\texttt{BealFinal/Main.lean:30--36,53,74--80}).
The v38 certificate \texttt{beal-v38 EQUIV:3} \recordid{792b3f8},
\texttt{chartOfModelTrue\_injective\_from\_Ei\_constraint},
displays \texttt{Function.Injective chartOfModelTrue} with
$E_i$ constraint and annihilator presentations, not the general proof.
Its chain is \recordid{ddfb2642} $\to$ \recordid{e466e5a} $\to$
\recordid{792b3f8}; the freeze object is \recordid{437b4c85}
at \recordid{c48b1bd3}.

A separate cover addresses $62500$ values $B\equiv14\pmod{16}$
below $10^6$; extension to all $B$ retains a Baker-bound premise.
Finite level-$26$ data are not modularity or level lowering.

Boundary: \texttt{conductor_86} and \texttt{level_lowering_86}
remain uninhabited Props, as does the \texttt{B14_honest} universal pack.
\texttt{beal_4_13_13_gap3_B_le_2M_eliminated} uses
\texttt{Classical.em}, not elimination.
Infinite density, full modular contradiction, and unrestricted Beal
remain open. \texttt{baker_bound_gap3} is not closed by
\texttt{smallZsigPrimes}. Bounded v33 gluing gives neither global special-fibre
identification nor degree-one/nonvanishing of $\overline{2t}$.
\end{abstract}
