\section{Displayed data III --- Ribet-style numerals for level
$32$, and a conditional trace lemma at $29$}\label{sec:ribet}

\subsection*{The packaged display}

The file
\texttt{Ribet\_Level\_Lowering\_29\_to\_32\_final.lean}
(\texttt{5631fd1}; incremental
\href{https://github.com/DavidFox998/beal-level-26-foundations/actions/runs/35489274297}{35489274297},
$282$s) displays the residual-level arithmetic named after
Ribet~\cite{ribet1990} without inhabiting abstract level lowering.

\begin{displaythm}[Ribet numerals]
\label{disp:ribet}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em]
\item $928/29=32$, $\ 32\cdot 29=928$, $\ \neg\,29\mid 32$;
\item $2^4=16$, $\ 16\mid 32$;
\item displayed $\dim S_2(\Ga_0(32))=1$, displayed
  $\dim S_2(\Ga_0(16))=0$;
\item Sturm pair $32\cdot\tfrac32=48$ and $2\cdot 48/12=8$;
\item $(\emptyset:\mathrm{Finset}\,\mathbb{N}).\mathrm{card}=0$.
\end{itemize}
The displayed full/new dimensions and the Sturm pair are the LMFDB
pin \texttt{full=1}, \texttt{new=1}, \texttt{level16=0},
\texttt{sturm=8} for the unique newform \texttt{32a1}~\cite{lmfdb}.
\end{displaythm}

The single named theorem
\texttt{\seqsplit{displayed\_level32\_arithmetic\_and\_newspace\_data}}
re-exports \texttt{no\_newforms\_at\_32}: the numerals of
Display~\ref{disp:ribet} together with
\texttt{displayed\_frey\_matching\_newforms.card $= 0$}. That
Finset is defined by \texttt{:= $\emptyset$}, not computed from the
Frey curve's traces, so its cardinality theorem is immediate and
proves nothing about which newform the Frey curve matches.

Earlier releases (through \texttt{v0.30}) exported this same
conjunction under three further names, \texttt{Ribet\_928\_to\_32},
\texttt{no\_newforms\_at\_32\_mod13}, and

below, \texttt{no\_match\_32a1\_of\_good\_red\_29} and
\texttt{no\_sol\_with\_good\_red\_29\_of\_trace\_match}, describe
their actual conditional conclusions and are kept unchanged.
\end{honesty}

\begin{honesty}[Newspace dimension $1$, not $0$]
LMFDB records $\dim S_2(\Ga_0(32))^{\mathrm{new}}=1$, with unique
newform \texttt{32a1}. The pasted Sage line
\texttt{CuspForms(32,2).new\_subspace().dimension()=0} is
\emph{not} a theorem. The honest Lean display is the empty

\subsection*{A separate, genuinely computed lemma:
\texttt{no\_match\_32a1\_of\_good\_red\_29}}

The file \texttt{Level32Table.lean}, imported by
\texttt{Ribet\_Level\_Lowering\_29\_to\_32\_final.lean} but not used
by any of the theorems above, contains a lemma that \emph{is}
derived rather than asserted:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:no-match}
\begin{aligned}
  &\texttt{no\_match\_32a1\_of\_good\_red\_29}\ \{A\ B:\mathbb N\}\\
  &\quad (\mathrm{hsol}: A^4+B^4=(B+3)^{13})\ (\mathrm{hA}:29\nmid A)\
    (\mathrm{hB}:29\nmid B)\ (\mathrm{hC}:29\nmid B+3):\\
  &\qquad \mathrm{frey\_ap}\ 29\ (A\bmod 29)\ (B\bmod 29)\neq(-10:\mathbb Z).
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Here $\mathrm{frey\_ap}\ p\ A\ B$ is the trace $a_p=p-\#E_{A,B}(\F_p)$
of the naive reduction of~\eqref{eq:frey} mod $p$, computed by
counting affine points, and $-10$ is \texttt{curve32a1\_ap\_29}, the
$a_{29}$ coefficient of \texttt{32a1}. The proof reduces
\eqref{eq:no-match} to a closed instance,
\texttt{frey\_a29\_ne\_32a1\_fin}: for every pair
$(A,B)\in(\Z/29\Z)^2$ with $A\neq 0$, $B\neq 0$,
$A^4+B^4\not\equiv 0\pmod{29}$, and
$A^4+B^4\equiv(B+3)^{13}\pmod{29}$ --- i.e.\ every residue pair
consistent with a good-reduction solution of~\eqref{eq:gap3} at
$29$ --- the trace is $\neq -10$. This is a finite check over the
$29^2=841$ candidate pairs, proved by \texttt{native\_decide}, not
\texttt{decide}.

\begin{honesty}[Axiom profile differs from the four named displays]
Rebuilding \texttt{Level32Table.lean} on the pinned toolchain and
running \texttt{\#print axioms no\_match\_32a1\_of\_good\_red\_29}
reports
\[
  [\mathtt{propext},\ \mathtt{Classical.choice},\
    \mathtt{Lean.ofReduceBool},\ \mathtt{Quot.sound}].
\]
The presence of \texttt{Lean.ofReduceBool} is exactly the mark of
\texttt{native\_decide}: this lemma trusts the compiled kernel
reduction of a finite check over $29^2=841$ residue pairs, unlike
every named-display theorem in Sections~\ref{sec:tate}--\ref{sec:kolyvagin},
which are stated above to avoid \texttt{native\_decide} and to have
no \texttt{Lean.ofReduceBool} in their axiom lists. Both facts are
true simultaneously: the four packaged \texttt{\_final} displays
really do avoid \texttt{native\_decide}, and this separate,
uncited lemma really does use it. Citing
\texttt{no\_match\_32a1\_of\_good\_red\_29} as part of an
axiom-clean argument would be inaccurate.
\end{honesty}

Under its stated hypotheses --- a gap-3 solution with good
reduction at $29$ on all of $A$, $B$, and $B+3$ --- the lemma is a
real, mechanically checked mod-$29$ trace mismatch, and the
assessment in this draft's first version understated it. It does
not, however, complete an elimination argument for three reasons
recorded in Section~\ref{sec:intro}: (i) it says nothing about
$29\mid ABC$; (ii) no Lean theorem shows a hypothetical solution of
the gap-3 family must have good reduction at $29$, so the hypothesis
is not known to hold in any case of interest; and (iii) the
identification of $a_{29}$ of the Frey curve's Galois representation
with $a_{29}$ of \texttt{32a1} --- which is what a completed
Ribet/level-lowering argument would supply --- is not derived here;
\texttt{ribet\_lowers\_Frey\_to\_level\_32} stays an uninhabited
\texttt{def Prop} (Section~\ref{sec:formal}). The companion theorem
\texttt{no\_sol\_with\_good\_red\_29\_of\_trace\_match} makes this
last gap explicit: it takes the trace-match hypothesis that Ribet
would need to supply as an additional argument and derives
\texttt{False} from it via \texttt{no\_match\_32a1\_of\_good\_red\_29}.
Its axiom list,
$[\mathtt{propext},\mathtt{Classical.choice},\mathtt{Lean.ofReduceBool},
\mathtt{Quot.sound}]$ (checked the same way), still carries
\texttt{Lean.ofReduceBool}, since it calls the
\texttt{native\_decide}-based lemma directly rather than merely
citing its statement; no theorem in this file removes that
dependency.
