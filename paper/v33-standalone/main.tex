\begin{abstract}
This working v33 manuscript records a three-open \texttt{OpenCover} of the
\emph{quotient} Rees scheme $\operatorname{Proj}(\mathrm{ReesMod2})$ by
$D_+(\overline{2t})$, $D_+(\overline{Xt})$, and $D_+(\overline{Yt})$,
with affine restriction squares for every ordered pair over abstract
product-open pullbacks. \texttt{CompatChart2t} (191 lines) identifies the
principal divided-chart kernel $(F)$ and proves
\texttt{chart\_eq\_quotient\_2t};
\texttt{CompatChartXt} and \texttt{CompatChartYt} (105 lines each)
prove $J_X=(G_X:X^\infty)$ and $J_Y=(G_Y:Y^\infty)$ and their
quotient-induced chart identifications. Six directed polynomial-to-abstract
restrictions satisfy $\mathrm{res}_{\mathrm{poly}}
=\mathrm{res}_{\mathrm{actual}}\circ\psi_\bullet$.
\texttt{SpecialFibreGluing} (167 lines) checks six composed
$\operatorname{Spec.map}$ compatibilities and the abstract triple
cocycle, giving
\[
\operatorname{Proj}(\mathrm{ReesMod2})\cong
\operatorname{Glue}(S_{2t}/(2),S_{Xt}/(2),S_{Yt}/(2)).
\]
Product overlaps are abstract pullbacks, not polynomial presentations.
For $y^2+xy=x^3+8$, the reduced $2t$ chart is $V^2+UV=0$;
the point $U=V=0$ lies only in $D_+(\overline{2t})$ (an
illustration, not a separately named Lean theorem).
\texttt{TateEvenBranchGenericFibre} (59 lines) packages the
\emph{actual} blow-up comparison
$\operatorname{Bl}_I[1/2]\cong\operatorname{Spec}(R_Z[1/2])$
with $I[1/2]=\top$; the existing 2,249-line chart file is untouched.
The seven targeted \texttt{lake env lean} direct checks previously passed
with printed axioms only \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice},
and \texttt{Quot.sound}; the scoped \texttt{sorry} scan and
\texttt{git diff --check} are clean. A standalone direct check of the
large special-fibre file timed out without a diagnostic and a full
\texttt{lake build BealGeneral} remains unverified. We do \emph{not}
claim a global isomorphism from the actual special-fibre pullback to
the quotient Rees $\operatorname{Proj}$, or a degree-one quotient
formula or nonvanishing theorem for $\overline{2t}$. This is not a
general Beal proof. The intended release and Zenodo source is
\texttt{beal-conjecture}, not its level-26 work mirror; its active
GitHub-side Zenodo \texttt{release} webhook does not yet establish

\section{Scope and charts}
Write $R_Z=\mathbb Z_2[X,Y]/(f)$ for the translated even-branch
surface under the stated nodal and coefficient-divisibility
hypotheses, $I=(2,X,Y)$, and
$\mathrm{ReesMod2}=\mathrm{Rees}(I)/(2)$, where $(2)$ is generated
by the degree-zero base scalar, \emph{not} by $2t$.
The integral divided chart has $f(2U,2V)=4F$;
its principal relation is $(F)$.
The $Xt$ and $Yt$ charts use saturated graph ideals
$(G_X:X^\infty)$ and $(G_Y:Y^\infty)$ rather than the
unsaturated graph equations. Quotient-induced chart maps connect
these integral calculations to the three reduced chart spectra.
These assertions concern the charts under their hypotheses,
not an unconditional formula for every Weierstrass model.

\section{The checked gluing boundary}
The three reduced generator opens cover
$\operatorname{Proj}(\mathrm{ReesMod2})$.
Ordered affine restriction squares live over product opens; the
six distinct directed pairs obey the polynomial-to-abstract
restriction identities. The polynomial spectra are literal cover
objects, but their \emph{overlaps} are abstract product pullbacks.
Six $\operatorname{Spec.map}$ squares and a triple cocycle yield the
global isomorphism displayed in the abstract. No polynomial ring
presentation of a product overlap is supplied or inferred.

The illustrative $y^2+xy=x^3+8$ specialization has
$f(2U,2V)=4(V^2+UV-2U^3-2)$, so its reduced divided-chart
equation is $V^2+UV=0$. The point $(U,V)=(0,0)$ gives direction
$[2t:Xt:Yt]=[1:0:0]$ and belongs to the $2t$ open alone;
this is an example, not a global classification theorem.

\section{Generic fibre and unresolved special-fibre comparison}
After inverting $2$, the centre $I$ becomes the unit ideal.
The packaged generic-fibre isomorphism concerns the \emph{actual}
blow-up and the localized translated surface over $D(2)$;
it does not identify an integral chart with its generic fibre.
In contrast, the checked special-fibre gluing concerns the
\emph{quotient Rees Proj}. Chartwise base-change comparisons do not
yet give an isomorphism of that Proj with the actual
scheme-theoretic special-fibre pullback. Nor is
$(\mathrm{ReesMod2})_1\cong(I/2I)t$ or
$\overline{2t}\ne0$ certified here. These missing steps are
not used as premises of the quotient-Proj gluing.
