\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conc}

This draft records, honestly, what four Lean~4.12 files named
after Tate, Mazur, Ribet, and Kolyvagin actually contain, assembled
in a development aimed at the restricted gap-3 family
$A^4+B^4=(B+3)^{13}$: numerals and Finset cardinalities, not proofs
of the classical theorems. Tate: $928=2^5\cdot 29$ at the test
parameters $(A,B)=(29,1)$ only ($I_0^*$, $I_8$), not a Lean N\'eron
conductor for general $(A,B)$. Mazur: $2184$ and cusp card~$2$
versus genus~$0$ --- no $13$-isogeny is excluded. Ribet:
$928/29=32$ with an \emph{asserted}, not derived, matching Finset
$\emptyset$ against LMFDB \texttt{32a1} of dimension~$1$. Kolyvagin:
$\lvert\Sel_2\rvert=1$, $3\cdot 7=21$, $L/\cO=1/3$ and $1/7$ on
\texttt{26a1}/\texttt{26b1}, where the repository's surrogate
\texttt{IsSubsingleton := Subsingleton} (renamed, as of this
revision, from \texttt{IsRankZero}) is not the conventional
algebraic-rank-zero predicate, and its application to each of these
curves is false, because each curve has nontrivial torsion.
Separately, and more substantively, the repository proves
\texttt{no\_match\_32a1\_of\_good\_red\_29}
(Section~\ref{sec:ribet}): a genuine, \texttt{native\_decide}-checked
mod-$29$ trace mismatch for gap-3 solutions with good reduction at
$29$, whose hypotheses are not shown to hold anywhere and which is
not linked in Lean to Ribet's level-lowering step. That lemma, not
the four asserted displays, is the closest thing in this repository
to a mathematically meaningful elimination result, and it is
scoped to a single prime ($p=29$), one local modulus, one newform
coefficient, and one restricted equation family (all $29^2=841$
residue pairs are checked, subject to filtering conditions; the
scoping is in the prime and the family, not in the number of
residues examined), not to Beal's conjecture or even to the
unrestricted signature $(4,4,13)$.

On this basis the draft is not a proof or a partial proof of any
instance of Beal's conjecture. It is a record of what a specific
Lean development does and does not establish, offered so that the
distinction between a displayed numeral and a mathematical theorem
is not lost in translation between the repository and its
description. The software versions are
\texttt{v30.0.0-level-26-structural-verification} on
\texttt{beal-conjecture} (\texttt{af520d3}, About tip
\texttt{ea261b9}) and
\texttt{v0.30-mcom-structural-verification} on foundations
\texttt{2b60ccd}. This revision additionally renames the
misleadingly-named Lean declarations discussed above (Tate, Mazur,
Ribet, and Kolyvagin displayed-data files, and
\texttt{IsRankZero}$\to$\texttt{IsSubsingleton} on
\texttt{BSD\_MordellWeil.lean}), with no compatibility alias kept
for the removed names; it is tagged as a new release,
\texttt{v31}, rather than silently replacing \texttt{v30}. Phase
stays \texttt{2b60ccd}. Tags \texttt{v0.27}--\texttt{v0.30} stay
live and continue to refer to the pre-rename declaration names.
Parent algebraic statements remain \texttt{def Prop}. No remaining
timers.
