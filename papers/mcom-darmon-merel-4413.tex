\begin{abstract}
We record a Lean~4 step toward a formalization of the Darmon--Merel method for $a^4+b^4=c^{13}$.
The curve is Pacetti's Frey $\mathbb Q$-curve over $\mathbb Q(i)$.
Darmon--Merel enters as Lemma~8.2, the missing contradiction behind one frontier proposition.
The special-fiber blow-up is counted: seven components when $a$ is odd, and nine when $a$ is even.
Only if the two propositions that identify those counts with the N\'eron special fiber are assumed does Pacetti's Artin exponent become a theorem.
The mod-$13$ residual and the $\mathbb Q$-curve extension are defined.
Two theorems project the existing axioms onto that package and do not close either frontier proposition.
For coprime $a,b$ both nonzero, nonexistence stays conditional on those two fiber propositions, on the absence of a newform of level $32$ or $64$, on an opaque irreducibility hypothesis, and on two named axioms.
A further trace divisibility implies the newform proposition and is not assumed by the capstone.
The five propositions named below are that frontier.
They are not every open proposition in the library.
There is no \texttt{sorry}.
This is not a proof of $a^4+b^4=c^{13}$, and it is not a proof of the Beal conjecture.
\end{abstract}
\maketitle

\section{What is formalized}
The curve is Pacetti's model for $x^4+dy^2=z^p$ at $d=1$ and $p=13$, with $x=a$, $y=b^2$, and $z=c$.
Darmon--Merel enters later, as Lemma~8.2: the missing \texttt{False} behind \texttt{no\_newform\_32\_64\_open}.
The equation
\[
y^2 = X^3 + 4a X^2 + 2\bigl(a^2 + b^2\sqrt{-1}\bigr) X
\]
has discriminant $\Delta = 2^9(a^2+b^2\sqrt{-1})\,c^{13}$.
The checked library is the branch and tag named in the thanks.
\texttt{lake build} and \texttt{lake build Mathlib} are the two build targets.
The printed axioms of the capstone \texttt{no\_solution\_4413\_of\_frontier} are the two named axioms \texttt{frey\_qcurve\_modular} and \texttt{ribet\_level\_lowering\_4413}, together with \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, and \texttt{Quot.sound}.
There is no \texttt{sorry} in the library.

The pairs $(1,0,1)$ and $(0,1,1)$ satisfy the equation, with $\gcd=1$ and $c>0$.
They are excluded by the quantifiers $a\neq 0$ and $b\neq 0$.
The pair $(8,8,2)$ fails coprimality.
There is no theorem that $a$ and $b$ cannot both be nonzero.

\section{The two component counts}
For odd $a$, the charts in \texttt{Mathlib/Modular/BlowupOddA.lean} give
\[
\texttt{OddAComponent.all.length} = 7
\]
(\texttt{length\_seven}).
Three components have multiplicity two: \texttt{chain1}, \texttt{theta}, and \texttt{chain2}.
Four have multiplicity one.
For even $a$, \texttt{BlowupEvenA.lean} gives
\[
\texttt{EvenAComponent.all.length} = 9
\]
(\texttt{length\_nine}, \texttt{even\_a\_nine\_components}).
Five components have multiplicity two.
Four have multiplicity one.
In \texttt{ComponentsOfKodairaNeron.lean} the same totals are the arithmetic identities $7=2+5$ and $9=4+5$.
Those sums are not multiplicity splits.

\texttt{kodaira\_neron\_odd\_a\_open} and \texttt{kodaira\_neron\_even\_a\_open} are the propositions that identify these counts with the opaque constant \texttt{NeronSpecialFiberComponents}.
They are hypotheses of the capstone, not theorems.
Local minimality at the prime above $2$ is proved.
\texttt{minimalAt2\_of\_c\_odd} and \texttt{minimalAt2\_of\_coprime} prove the predicate \texttt{qiLocalMinimalAtPi}, for odd $c$ and for a coprime pair.
Minimality in the sense of a N\'eron model is not proved.
Adjacency of the components, normal crossings, and the identification of the N\'eron model with the smooth locus are not proved.

\section{The rewired Pacetti path}
Write $\pi$ for a prime element above $2$ in $\mathbb Z[\sqrt{-1}]$.
\texttt{FTameAtPi} and \texttt{SwanAtPi} are opaque: the library does not unfold them to equations.
\texttt{pacetti\_2\_8\_open} is a live proposition, not a frontier hypothesis of the capstone.
On a coprime curve, it says that the sum of those two conductors lies in $\{10,12\}$.
It is not a theorem by itself.
\texttt{pacetti\_of\_kodaira\_neron} proves it only from \texttt{kodaira\_neron\_odd\_a\_open} and \texttt{kodaira\_neron\_even\_a\_open}.
The capstone calls that theorem and passes the resulting term to the level-lowering axiom.
It calls no other derivation of the exponent.
\texttt{pacetti\_of\_components} still derives the same sentence from the separate open \texttt{components\_n\_plus\_five\_open}.
The capstone does not assume that open and does not call that theorem.

\texttt{FreyConductorDivides} is the disjunction $N=32|c|^2$ or $N=64|c|^2$.
The capstone passes the first branch, by \texttt{Or.inl rfl}, with $N=32|c|^2$.
The predicate is not the $32$-shape alone, and it is not a computation of the conductor.

\section{The Galois skeleton}
\texttt{DarmonMerel/8\_Galois4413.lean} defines the residual package and the coefficient extension.
It also proves \texttt{frey\_qcurve\_modular\_on\_RhoE13\_holds}.
\par
It also proves \texttt{ribet\_level\_lowering\_on\_RhoE13\_holds}.
\par
Those two theorems project \texttt{frey\_qcurve\_modular} and \texttt{ribet\_level\_lowering\_4413}.
They do not close \texttt{RhoE13Open} or \texttt{qcurve\_extension\_169\_open}.
The file declares no \texttt{axiom} and uses no \texttt{sorry}.

\texttt{structure RhoE13} is stated for a \texttt{FreyCurve4413}.

The representation space is \texttt{RhoE13Space.unresolved}.
\par
The residual is \texttt{rho\_E\_p}.
\par
That residual is \texttt{GaloisRepresentation.unresolved}.
There is no formalization of $\mathrm{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb Q}/\mathbb Q(i))$ and none of $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb F_{13})$.
\texttt{RhoE13.ofFrey} builds that unresolved package.
It does not produce a term of \texttt{RhoE13Open}.
\texttt{rhoE13\_irreducible\_open} is \texttt{RhoE13Open}.

\texttt{structure qcurve\_extension\_169} stores the conjugate of the linear coefficient and the equality \texttt{velu\_target\_eq\_twist\_neg\_two}.
Both sides of that equality are the coefficient pair $(-8a,\, 8a^2+8b^2\sqrt{-1})$.
The equality is the coefficient form of the $2$-isogeny from the Galois conjugate to the twist by $-2$.
It is not a class in a cohomology group and not a map on a Tate module.
\texttt{qcurve\_extension\_169.ofFrey} fills those fields from the proved coefficient theorems.
It does not prove \texttt{qcurve\_extension\_169\_open}.
\texttt{qcurve\_extension\_169\_cocycle\_open} is that proposition.

\texttt{frey\_qcurve\_modular\_on\_RhoE13} states what \texttt{frey\_qcurve\_modular} gives.
The conclusion is \texttt{Modular E}, the residual of \texttt{RhoE13.ofFrey} left unresolved, and a nonempty coefficient extension.
\texttt{ribet\_level\_lowering\_on\_RhoE13} states what \texttt{ribet\_level\_lowering\_4413} gives.
Its hypotheses are \texttt{FreyConductorDivides}, a term of \texttt{RhoE13Open}, and \texttt{pacetti\_2\_8\_open}.
The conclusion is a nonempty \texttt{RibetLevel}, the proposition \texttt{GkResidualIso}, and the same unresolved residual.
\texttt{RibetLevel} is the package returned by the level-lowering axiom: a level $n$ with $n=32$ or $n=64$, and one lowered newform.
\texttt{GkResidualIso} says that, for a coprime modular curve carrying such a package, with both residual markers unresolved, $13$ divides \texttt{freyTrace3}$-(-6)$.
Neither statement constructs a representation.

\section{Frontier propositions}
Five propositions are the frontier this note tracks.
The capstone is conditional on the first four, together with the two axioms.
The fifth is not a hypothesis of the capstone.
None of the five is an \texttt{axiom}, and none is \texttt{sorry}.
They are not every open proposition in the library.
Further opens that the capstone does not assume include \texttt{components\_n\_plus\_five\_open}, \texttt{serre\_two\_level\_open}, \texttt{odd\_level\_one\_open}, \texttt{rhoE13\_open\_of\_psi}, and \texttt{dickson\_13\_open}.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \texttt{kodaira\_neron\_odd\_a\_open}, as above. Hypothesis of the capstone.
\item \texttt{kodaira\_neron\_even\_a\_open}, as above. Hypothesis of the capstone.
\item \texttt{no\_newform\_32\_64\_open} is defined in file~3, \texttt{DarmonMerel/3\_RibetLowering4413.lean}, immediately after \texttt{structure RibetLevel} and before \texttt{GkResidualIso}.
The capstone applies it once, to the package returned by the level-lowering axiom.
It is the missing \texttt{False} from Darmon--Merel Lemma~8.2. The Mazur products at the newforms $32.2.a.a$ and $64.2.a.a$ are $0$, and $(T_5-6)e\not\equiv 0\pmod{13}$ at both levels, so those two calculations do not yet contradict each other.
\item \texttt{RhoE13Open}, an \texttt{opaque} proposition with no body, in the same file.
Nothing in the repository produces a term. The level-lowering axiom does not apply without one.
The carrier is \texttt{RhoE13}, described above.
\item \texttt{qcurve\_extension\_169\_open} is the divisibility $13\mid \texttt{freyTrace3}-(-6)$, under coprimality, modularity, a \texttt{RibetLevel}, the V\'elu/twist equality, and $13\nmid 2$.
The definition does not assert that a representation exists.
$\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb F_{169})$ is not a defined object in the library.
\texttt{no\_newform\_32\_64\_of\_qcurve} derives item~3 from this sentence.
The capstone does not assume it.
The structure \texttt{qcurve\_extension\_169} does not prove the divisibility.

\texttt{thirteen\_not\_dvd\_two} is the integrality $13\nmid 2$.
It is a theorem in file~3 and is used by the isogeny input of that derivation.
\end{enumerate}

\section{The two axioms}
\texttt{frey\_qcurve\_modular} produces \texttt{Modular E}.

\texttt{ribet\_level\_lowering\_4413} is a structure \texttt{RibetLowering}.

Its level field returns a \texttt{RibetLevel}.
The three inputs are \texttt{FreyConductorDivides}, a term of \texttt{RhoE13Open}, and \texttt{pacetti\_2\_8\_open}.
Its other field is \texttt{GkResidualIso}.
The disjunction $n=32\lor n=64$ is part of that axiom. It is not deduced from the component counts.
The skeleton does not add an axiom.

\section{Closing}
The library is a step toward the Darmon--Merel method for the signature $a^4+b^4=c^{13}$: the curve, the component counts, the exponent conditional on the two fiber propositions, the Galois skeleton, and the conditional capstone.
It is not a proof of $a^x+b^y=c^z$ for general exponents, and it is not a proof of $a^4+b^4=c^{13}$.
Whether that step is published is the editors' decision.
A DOI for the repository is a separate question.
The default branch \texttt{main} is a different history.
This branch still carries the two axioms, the five frontier propositions above, and further opens the capstone does not assume.
The only manuscript in this branch is this file.
