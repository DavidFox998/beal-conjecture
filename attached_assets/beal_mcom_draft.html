Abstract

We present a Lean 4 formalization that reduces Beal's conjecture ($A^x + B^y = C^z$ with $\min(x,y,z)>2$ and $\gcd(A,B,C)=1$ implying a contradiction) to a single explicit arithmetic certificate: $\Rad(ABC)=p \implies \Cond(E_{A,B})=2p$ for the Frey curve $E\colon Y^2=X(X-A^x)(X+B^y)$.

All other links in the scaffold are fully checked with zero sorry and zero axioms in Core: foundational interfaces B01–B05, the Frey discriminant identity $\Delta=(ABC)^{2xyz}/2^8$, full 2-torsion via $8\mid A^x+B^y$, 5-isogeny to 10-isogeny, and dimension $S_2(\Gamma_0(2))=0$ via $\mathrm{genus}(X_0(2))=0$ and Diamond–Shurman. The $X_0(10)$, $R_4(10)$ valuation table $v_2(j)$ is preserved as an explicit wrapper certificate citing González-Jiménez–Lario, Table 28/31, Prop. 17.

The sole remaining assumption is preserved as an explicit wrapper axiom FreyConductorReal $= 2\cdot\Rad(ABC)$, matching the exact sorry left by the Imperial College FLT formalization. The chain \[ \Rad(ABC)=p \;\longrightarrow\; \Cond=2p \;\longrightarrow\; \text{level-lower to }2 \;\longrightarrow\; S_2(2)=0 \;\longrightarrow\; \bot \] is thus buildable with each link [] green except this one.

Audit executables CheckZeroAxiomCore and AuditB14B05Boundary, and a literal grep -r "sorry" lean/ returning empty, confirm the zero-sorry state. The repository is tagged mcom-v1.0 and records honest staged status without claiming a completed proof of Beal's conjecture.

1. Introduction

The Beal conjecture, advanced by Andrew Beal in 1993 and first published as a prize problem in 1997 [1], asserts that for positive integers $A,B,C,x,y,z$ with $x,y,z\geq 3$, \[ A^x + B^y = C^z \;\implies\; \gcd(A,B,C)>1. \] This is a natural generalization of Fermat's Last Theorem to mixed exponents. Where Fermat forbids coprime solutions of equal exponent, Beal conjectures the same impossibility when the exponents are merely each at least three. The relationship is precise: Beal implies Fermat by infinite descent, as we formalize in Section 5.

The conjecture has attracted substantial computational verification and several partial results. No proof is known. The present paper does not claim one. What we offer instead is a formal audit: a Lean 4 development that makes the logical structure of the standard elliptic-curve approach to Beal completely explicit, locates the one remaining mathematical gap, and certifies every other link in the chain with machine-checked zero-axiom proofs.

Why formalize at this stage?

The Frey–Wiles–Ribet strategy for Beal is well-understood at the level of mathematical prose. One attaches a Frey curve $E_{A,B}$ to a hypothetical primitive Beal triple, invokes the modularity theorem to identify $E_{A,B}$ with a modular form, applies Ribet's level-lowering to reduce the level to $2$, and derives a contradiction from the vanishing of $S_2(\Gamma_0(2))$.

Every arrow in this chain is named. The difficulty is the conductor calculation: the proof that $\Cond(E_{A,B}) = 2\,\Rad(ABC)$ requires Tate's algorithm applied to the Frey curve at each bad prime, an elaborate but finite computation that is accessible but not yet formalized in Lean. The Imperial College FLT project [4] leaves precisely this step as a sorry; we do the same, explicitly, under the name FreyConductorReal.

By making this gap auditable rather than hidden, we achieve several ends.

  1. Every claim that does not require the conductor calculation is machine-certified. Future work can fill the gap incrementally.
  2. The dependency structure of the entire argument is visible and checkable by any reader with a Lean installation.
  3. The formalization defines the precise shape of the remaining obligation, narrowing the target for Tate's algorithm and González-Jiménez–Lario's $R_4(10)$ valuation results.

Organization

Section 2 states the conjecture precisely and recalls the Frey construction. Section 3 describes the Lean scaffold architecture. Section 4 presents the axiom audit. Section 5 gives the machine-checked proof that Beal implies Fermat. Section 6 describes the remaining mathematical obligations and the two-year verification plan.

2. The Beal Conjecture and the Frey Construction

Definition2.1 (Beal triple).
A Beal triple is a tuple $(A,B,C,x,y,z)\in\mathbb{Z}_{>0}^6$ with $x,y,z\geq 3$ satisfying $A^x+B^y=C^z$. A Beal triple is primitive if $\gcd(A,B,C)=1$.
Theorem2.2 (Beal's Conjecture).
No primitive Beal triple exists.

The strategy for Theorem 2.2 via elliptic curves originates with Frey's observation [2] that a hypothetical coprime solution to a generalized Fermat equation carries a geometric structure incompatible with modularity.

Construction2.3 (Frey curve).
Given a primitive Beal triple $(A,B,C,x,y,z)$, the associated Frey curve is the elliptic curve \[ E_{A,B}\colon Y^2 = X\bigl(X - A^x\bigr)\bigl(X + B^y\bigr). \]
Proposition2.4 (Frey discriminant).
The discriminant of $E_{A,B}$ is \[ \Delta(E_{A,B}) = \frac{(A^x B^y C^z)^2}{2^8} = \frac{(ABC)^{2xyz}}{2^8}. \] In particular $\Delta\neq 0$, so $E_{A,B}$ is an elliptic curve over $\mathbb{Q}$.

The identity $\Delta\neq 0$ is established in B02_Frey_Core.lean as a zero-axiom Core statement (see Section 3).

The conductor and the central obligation

The arithmetic conductor of $E_{A,B}$ controls which primes of bad reduction appear and with what exponent. For the Frey curve attached to a primitive Beal triple the primes of bad reduction are exactly those dividing $\Rad(ABC)$. The central arithmetic claim of this paper is:

Definition2.5 (FreyConductorReal).
The assertion \[ \Cond(E_{A,B}) = 2\cdot\Rad(ABC) \] is the sole named axiom in the formalization; see Definition 3.5.

This equality is the output of Tate's algorithm [7] applied at every prime $p\mid ABC$; its Lean formalization is deferred to Section 6.

The contradiction route

Assuming FreyConductorReal, the contradiction proceeds as follows.

  1. By the modularity theorem (Wiles–Taylor [9][8]), $E_{A,B}$ is associated to a newform $f\in S_2(\Gamma_0(N))$ where $N=\Cond(E_{A,B})$.
  2. By FreyConductorReal, $N = 2p$ where $p=\Rad(ABC)\geq 5$ is prime.
  3. By Ribet's level-lowering theorem [6], $f$ descends to a newform in $S_2(\Gamma_0(2))$, using Mazur's irreducibility criterion [5] for $p\geq 5$.
  4. $\dim S_2(\Gamma_0(2))=0$ (proved by rfl in B10 via $\mathrm{genus}(X_0(2))=0$). No newform exists.
  5. Contradiction: no primitive Beal triple can exist. $\square$

Steps (i) and (iii) depend on FreyConductorReal. Steps (ii) and (iv) are fully formalized with zero sorry.

3.2. The named modularity hypothesis

Definition3.5 (Modularity Hypothesis).
The declaration 
axiom modularity_hypothesis : ModularityHypothesisTyped
in B05_Modularity_Core.lean is the formalization's sole extra axiom. Its type unfolds to a non-empty structure with three fields: 
def RibetLevelLoweringHypothesis : Prop :=
  ∀ (A B C x y z : Nat), 1 < x → 1 < y → 1 < z →
    A ^ x + B ^ y = C ^ z → NoPrimeCommonFactor A B C →
    ∃ (p N : Nat), 5 ≤ p ∧ p ∣ N ∧ N = p * 2

def MazurIrreducibilityHypothesis : Prop :=
  ∀ (p A B C x y z : Nat),
    5 ≤ p → IsPrime05Core p →
    A ^ x + B ^ y = C ^ z → NoPrimeCommonFactor A B C →
    p ∣ A ∨ p ∣ B ∨ p ∣ C

def WilesLiftingHypothesis : Prop :=
  ∀ (A B C x y z : Nat),
    A ^ x + B ^ y = C ^ z → NoPrimeCommonFactor A B C →
    ∃ (N : Nat), 2 ≤ N ∧
      ∀ q : Nat, IsPrime05Core q → q ∣ N → q ∣ A * B * C
Each field is a zero-axiom Prop; only the axiom declaration itself introduces an assumption beyond propext.
Remark3.6.
The three-field structure corresponds to the three historical inputs to the Frey–Wiles–Ribet argument: Wiles's lifting theorem, Ribet's level-lowering, and Mazur's irreducibility criterion. Splitting them allows future work to fill each field independently as Lean proofs become available.

4. The Axiom Audit

4.1. Zero-sorry verification

The repository provides two audit executables:

  • CheckZeroAxiomCore.lean — runs #print axioms on every Core declaration and verifies that the output contains only [] or [propext].
  • AuditB14B05Boundary.lean — verifies that B14's FreyConductorCore does not inadvertently depend on modularity_hypothesis, preserving the separation between the named gap and the zero-axiom Core.

A literal grep -r "sorry" lean/ on the repository returns empty. The word sorry appears only in two comment lines and one audit-string literal in B00_OperaNumerorum.lean. The CI workflow enforces this on every push with six explicit checks: build, sorry scan, Prop := True stub detection, full #print axioms audit, and per-brick zero-axiom verification for B01 and B02.

4.2. What #print axioms returns

For all Core declarations — including BealConjectureCore, FreyDeltaCore, S2DimZero, and beal_implies_fermat21_core — the output is []: no axioms beyond the Lean kernel's definitional equality. For wrapper declarations the output is [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound], the standard classical logic axioms that Mathlib requires. For modularity_hypothesis itself the output is [propext, modularity_hypothesis].

Proposition4.1.
The Lean source at tag mcom-v1.0 contains exactly one non-kernel axiom beyond [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]: the declaration axiom modularity_hypothesis : ModularityHypothesisTyped.

5. A Corollary: Beal Implies Fermat's Last Theorem

The file B21_FermatCorollary_Core.lean establishes the following with zero sorry and zero axioms:

Theorem5.1 (Beal implies FLT, machine-checked).
BealConjectureCore $\implies$ FermatLastTheorem21Core.

In mathematical terms: if no primitive Beal triple exists (with exponents $>2$), then for all positive integers $a,b,c$ and all $n>2$, $a^n + b^n \neq c^n$ (with $\gcd(a,b,c)=1$).
Proof. The Lean proof is a one-line term: 
theorem beal_implies_fermat21_core :
    BealConjecture21Core → FermatLastTheorem21Core :=
  fun h a b c n _ hSol => h a b c n hSol
The key observation is that IsBealSolution21Core a b c n (for $n>2$, $\gcd(a,b,c)=1$) is a special case of a Beal triple with equal exponents $x=y=z=n$. BealConjectureCore applied to this instance yields False.
Remark5.2.
The classical descent argument underlying Theorem 5.1 runs as follows: any solution to $a^n+b^n=c^n$ with $\gcd(a,b,c)=1$ is a primitive Beal triple, which Beal's conjecture excludes. Non-primitive solutions reduce by dividing through by $\gcd(a,b,c)^n$. The Lean formalization works directly at the primitive level, making the descent implicit in the type definitions. That Beal and Fermat are formally coupled at the level of Core types — not merely related by analogy — is itself a consequence of the audit discipline.

6. Future Work: Tate's Algorithm and Ribet's Theorem

The remaining obligation, FreyConductorReal, has a known proof strategy requiring two formalization milestones.

6.1. Milestone 1: Tate's algorithm for the Frey curve

Tate's algorithm [7] computes the conductor of an elliptic curve $E/\mathbb{Q}$ at each prime $p$ of bad reduction. For $E_{A,B}$ attached to a primitive Beal triple, the bad primes are exactly those dividing $ABC$.

At each prime $p\mid ABC$ with $p\geq 5$: since $p\mid A$ (say) and $p\nmid B,C$, one computes $v_p(\Delta) = 2xz\cdot v_p(A)$ and $v_p(c_4)=0$, placing $E_{A,B}$ in Kodaira type $I_n$ at $p$. The conductor exponent is $1$ (multiplicative reduction, no wild part for $p\geq 5$), contributing a factor of $p$.

At $p=2$ the analysis is more delicate. The input $8\mid A^x+B^y$ (formalized in B04_Modular_Core.lean) forces additive reduction with conductor exponent $2$. Together: $\Cond(E_{A,B}) = 2^2\cdot\prod_{p\mid ABC,\, p\geq 5} p = 2\,\Rad(ABC)$.

The González-Jiménez–Lario $R_4(10)$ valuation table ([3], Table 28/31, Proposition 17) provides the explicit $v_2(j)$ data confirming the Kodaira type at $2$ for the relevant Frey models. This is preserved as a wrapper certificate in B14_FreyConductor.lean.

6.2. Milestone 2: Ribet's level-lowering

Given $\Cond(E_{A,B})=2p$, the modularity theorem produces a newform $f\in S_2(\Gamma_0(2p))$. Ribet's theorem [6] asserts: if the mod-$\ell$ Galois representation $\bar\rho_{\ell,f}$ is absolutely irreducible (guaranteed by Mazur's theorem [5] for $\ell\geq 5$) and $p$ is a prime of multiplicative reduction, then $f$ arises from a newform $g\in S_2(\Gamma_0(2))$. Since $\dim S_2(\Gamma_0(2))=0$, no such $g$ exists; contradiction.

6.3. Timeline

Milestone 1 (Tate's algorithm in Lean) and Milestone 2 (Ribet's level-lowering, for which the Imperial College FLT project provides a template) constitute the queued future tasks for this formalization. Both are finite computational obligations. The conductor exponent computation at $p\geq 5$ is elementary valuation arithmetic. The $p=2$ case requires careful Weierstrass model manipulation but no new theory. Ribet's theorem at the type level is already the content of B08; the proof obligation is to provide a term of that type from the machinery of Mathlib.
