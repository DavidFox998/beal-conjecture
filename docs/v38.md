### Membership of the ratio

`J_X` and `J_Y` are ideals of `R[T₀,T₁,T₂]`, not of the Rees
algebra. The element `2t` is not a term of that polynomial ring.
On the chart `D₊(Xᵢ t)` the degree-zero ratio `2t / (Xᵢ t)` is the
class of the chart variable `T₀`. That variable lies outside both
saturated ideals (`5bbe0458`):

* `two_t_ratio_not_mem_JX`: `T₀ ∉ J_X = (G_X : X^∞)`;
* `two_t_ratio_not_mem_JY`: `T₀ ∉ J_Y = (G_Y : Y^∞)`.

The proof is the saturation description together with regularity of
`2` and the fact that neither translated coordinate is nilpotent.
The `Y²` coefficient of the surface equation is `1`, and the `X³`
coefficient is `-1`, so a power of `X` or of `Y` cannot lie in the
principal ideal of the equation.

Workaround A does not need `O(1)`. On a point of the curve,
`overlineTwoT_pow_nonzero` says every power of the class is
nonzero in the graded ring `Rees / (2)`. A relation
`(2t)^n = 2 * f` puts `2^(n-1)` in the `n`-th power of the
centre; evaluation at the centre would make `2` a unit of
`ℤ_[2]`. The hypothesis is
`localWeierstrassEquation W x y = 0`. Off the curve the class
need not stay nonzero, so the unconditional formula is not the
theorem. The coefficients `Y² = 1` and `X³ = -1` are what make

The power theorem does not produce a global section. The open

`even_solution_implies_two_divides`.

## 2. `UniversallyClosed` for `Bl_I`

Needed, and not proved. Mathlib v4.12.0 has `UniversallyClosed`
and `IsProperMap`, and no valuative criterion. It has no
properness theorem for `Proj` of a finitely generated graded
algebra, and none for Rees `Proj`.

The source cover is jointly surjective.
`localSurfaceCentreReesGenerator_cover` says
`D₊(2t) ∪ D₊(Xt) ∪ D₊(Yt) = Proj`. Each basic open is affine.
`twoChart_finiteType` and `surfaceCoordinate_finiteType` are
finite type over `ℤ_[2]`. Finite type is not `UniversallyClosed`.
A cover of the source is not an open cover of the target.

Target locality was the next attempt.
`universallyClosed_isLocalAtTarget` reduces the predicate along
an open cover of the target. `Spec(ℤ_[2])` does not split into
the open `D(2)` and the closed set `V(2)`:

* `base_closedPoint_not_mem_D2`: the closed point `(2)` misses `D(2)`;
* `base_closedPoint_mem_open_iff_top`: an open contains that point
  if and only if the open is the whole spectrum;
* `base_V2_not_open`: `V(2)` is not open, and `base_V2_ne_univ`
  says it is not the whole space.

`{D(2), V(2)}` is therefore not an open cover, and
`IsLocalAtTarget.iff_of_iSup_eq_top` does not apply to it.

Over `D(2)`, `generic_fibre_iso` is an isomorphism of the pullback
with `Spec(R_Z[1/2])` over `D(2)`, and `generic_centre_eq_top` says
`I[1/2] = ⊤`. The isomorphism is not an identification with `D(2)`.
The structure map of that affine surface is not shown to be
universally closed.

Over `V(2)`, `7d48c25d` identifies the fibre with `Proj(Rees / (2))`.
That is not properness of the fibre and not properness of
`localSurfaceCentreReesToBase`. `overlineTwoT_pow_nonzero` (`8dbf1d6d`)
keeps powers of the class nonzero on a point of the curve. It does
not produce a specialization into the special fibre.

`UniversallyClosed` stays open. No instance is declared.
