### Membership of the ratio

`J_X` and `J_Y` are ideals of `R[T₀,T₁,T₂]`, not of the Rees
algebra. The element `2t` is not a term of that polynomial ring.
On the chart `D₊(Xᵢ t)` the degree-zero ratio `2t / (Xᵢ t)` is the
class of the chart variable `T₀`. That variable lies outside both
saturated ideals (`5bbe0458`):

* `two_t_ratio_not_mem_JX`: `T₀ ∉ J_X = (G_X : X^∞)`;
* `two_t_ratio_not_mem_JY`: `T₀ ∉ J_Y = (G_Y : Y^∞)`.

The proof is the saturation description together with regularity of
`2` and the fact that neither translated coordinate is nilpotent.
The `Y²` coefficient of the surface equation is `1`, and the `X³`
coefficient is `-1`, so a power of `X` or of `Y` cannot lie in the
principal ideal of the equation.
