## 1. Degree-one nonzero `overline{2t}` — PROVED identification

Needed, and excluded by v34. A nonzero degree-one section
`overline{2t}` on the special fibre is the missing input for the
two chart maps:

* even exponents: a map into the even chart, supplying
  `even_solution_implies_two_divides` and, with it, `even_Beal`
  (`2 ∣ X` and `2 ∣ Y`);
* odd exponents: a map into the odd chart, supplying
  `odd_branch_chart` (`2 ∤ X` and `2 ∤ Y`).

v35 does not provide that section. `odd_exponents_avoid_two_chart`
is the mod-4 fact `Odd x → Odd p → x^p % 4 = x % 4 → 2 ∤ x^p`.
It is not a chart map. The counterexample `Odd 3 ∧ 2 ∣ 2^3` shows
an odd exponent does not by itself keep a power out of `(2)`.
`I[1/2] = ⊤` is the generic fibre after inverting `2`, and it is
not applied to either map.

v36 proves `Nat.Coprime x y → ¬ (2 ∣ x ∧ 2 ∣ y)`. So the even
conclusion, once it existed for a coprime solution, would
contradict coprimeness. That contradiction is not available while
the even chart map is open. Coprimeness does not force both bases
odd: `Nat.Coprime 2 3`.
