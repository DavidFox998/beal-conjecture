## Computable level-26 foundations

This first release isolates the finite evidence used by the level-26 branch of
the companion `beal-conjecture` project.

Lean checks:

- the first two normalized coefficients of the `26a1` and `26b1` eigenlines;
- the coefficient matrix
  \[
  C_3=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\2&1\end{pmatrix};
  \]
- the explicit basis change
  \[
  P=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\1&1\end{pmatrix};
  \]
- the derived identity
  \[
  PC_3=M_3=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&2\end{pmatrix};
  \]
- \(\det(M_3)=2\ne0\) in \(\mathbf F_3\);
- the eight signed \(S\)-unit representatives and ten binary-quartic rows;
- ten untwisted finite-field bad-prime checks, repeated across the indexed
  carrier in this superseded release; and
- the fact that this finite audit retains all eight indices and is not a
  singleton Selmer computation.

The standard-library replay script separately checks 160 strong-Hensel
witnesses, covering every signed twist, quartic row, and prime \(2,13\).

## Exact boundary

This release does not prove that the finite candidate carrier is the genuine
cohomological \(2\)-Selmer group, does not infer Mordell--Weil rank, and does
not identify \(M_3\) with the actual Abel--Jacobi cotangent map at the cusp.
Those are named mathematical comparison theorems for later releases.

There are no `sorry`, `admit`, vacuous `True` declarations, or domain-specific
axioms in the Lean source.
