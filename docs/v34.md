## What v34 is

The even branch packages the checked Rees charts and describes the
reduced `2`-chart equation as a node: two lines through one point.
It does not replace that node by a projective line.

Proved in `FullReduction.lean`:

- For `y² + xy = x³ + 8`, the `2`-chart substitution `x = 2U`, `y = 2V` factors as `4(V² + UV - 2U³ - 2)`.
- `V² + UV = V(V + U)` over any commutative ring.
- Over a domain, the zero set equals the union of the lines `V = 0` and `V + U = 0`.
- Those lines meet only at `(0,0)`.
- Over `𝔽₂` the solutions are `(0,0)`, `(1,0)`, and `(1,1)`. The last two lie on exactly one line.
- In `Spec(ℤ_[2])`, the complement of `D(2)` is `V(2)`. Because `ℤ_[2]` is a domain and `2 ≠ 0`, `D(2)` is a nonempty dense open.
- The divided `2`-chart ring is a finite-type `ℤ_[2]`-algebra: a quotient of a polynomial ring in two variables.

## Excluded

- `UniversallyClosed` and topological `IsProperMap` for the Rees `Proj`. Mathlib v4.12.0 has both predicates and no properness theorem for this Rees algebra. Finite type of an affine chart is not universal closedness.
- `E ≅ ℙ¹_{𝔽₂}`. The reduced equation is a node, two lines, not a projective line.
- Identification of the actual special-fibre pullback with the quotient `Proj`.
- Degree one, or nonvanishing, of `overline{2t}` on that pullback.
- A chart map or specialization from an even solution of `x^p + y^q = z^r` to `D₊(2t)` or to the node. `even_Beal`, `even_solution_chart`, `even_solution_specializes_to_node`, and `even_solution_implies_two_divides` are uninhabited `def`s. No theorem concludes `2 ∣ X` and `2 ∣ Y` for a Tate model.
