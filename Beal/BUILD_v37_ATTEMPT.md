## What was proved

`overlineTwoT` is `Ideal.Quotient.mk` of `localSurfaceCentreReesTwo` by `localSurfaceCentreReesSpecialIdeal`. That ideal is `Ideal.span` of the degree-zero scalar `2`. It is not the ideal generated by `2t`.

* `overlineTwoT_mem_degree_one`: the class lies in degree one of the homogeneous quotient.
* `overlineTwoT_ne_zero`: if the surface equation lies in the centre, then `1` is outside the centre (`one_not_mem_centre_of_maximal`) and the scalar `2` does not kill `2t` (`scalar_two_does_not_kill_two_t`). The degree-one class is nonzero.
* `overlineTwoT_sq_ne_zero`: if the surface equation lies in the square of the centre, then `(2t)^2` is not in `(2)`. A relation `(2t)^2 = 2 * f` forces the degree-two coefficient of `f` to be the uniformizer, and Rees membership would put that uniformizer in the square of the centre. `localSurfaceUniformizer_not_mem_closedPoint_sq` says it is not.

No generator was found that kills `2t`. The degree-zero scalar `2` does not, and it does not kill `(2t)^2` either, under the second-order hypothesis. The failure condition "ReesMod2 degree-one is zero" does not hold.

`surfaceCentreBasicSpecialChartIso` identifies the actual pullback of one Rees basic open along `ℤ_[2] → ℤ/2ℤ` with the matching basic open of `Proj(Rees / (2))`. The reduced denominator may be nilpotent.

`twoAdicChartQuotientProjIso_factor` splits that isomorphism into the affine special-fibre isomorphism, `Spec` of `homogeneousTwoAdicAwayQuotientEquiv_any.symm`, and the inverse basic-open presentation. The factorization is definitional.

`twoAdicChartQuotientProjIso_toProduct` is the product-open square for that factorization. It pastes `homogeneousProjBasicTwoAdicAffine_toProduct`, the inverse of `homogeneousTwoAdicAwayQuotient_toProduct`, and the inverse of `homogeneousProjBasicSchemeIso_toProduct`. No nilpotence hypothesis is used.

`twoAdicPullbackInclusion_pasting` carries the product inclusion through `pullbackRightPullbackFstIso`. `twoAdicCoverChartIso_toProduct` is the same square after that pasting, which is the square `glueMorphisms` needs on each ordered pair of basic opens.
